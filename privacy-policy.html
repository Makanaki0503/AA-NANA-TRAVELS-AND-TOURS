Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

1. Introduction

AA Nana Travels and Tours Ltd ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

  • Full name and date of birth
  • Contact information (email, phone number, address)
  • Passport details and nationality
  • Payment information (credit card details, bank account)
  • Travel preferences and requirements
  • Medical information (for travel insurance or special assistance)

Technical Information:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information
  • Cookies and usage data
  • Pages visited and time spent on site

3. How We Collect Information

  • Booking forms and contact forms on our website
  • Phone calls and email communications
  • In-person consultations at our office
  • Social media interactions
  • Cookies and similar technologies
  • Third-party travel partners and service providers

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Process your travel bookings and payments
  • Communicate with you about your travel arrangements
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Send you newsletters and promotional offers (with consent)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and enhance security

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Airlines, hotels, and tour operators: To fulfill your travel bookings
  • Visa processing authorities: For visa applications
  • Payment processors: To handle transactions securely
  • Legal authorities: When required by law
  • Service providers: IT support, marketing, and analytics

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

  • SSL encryption on our website
  • Secure payment gateways
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • Staff training on data protection

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Object to processing of your information
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@aananatravels.com

8. Cookies Policy

  • Essential cookies: Required for website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors use our site
  • Marketing cookies: To personalize ads and content
